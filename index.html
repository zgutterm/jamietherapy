How I work

I'll collaborate with you to access or rediscover the capacities for healing within yourself. Healing and change happens within the context of a secure, responsive, attuned, and caring therapeutic relationship.

From that foundation, I will help you learn to decipher what your body and emotions are communicating to you, approach your emotional experiences with curiosity, and develop new ways of interacting with yourself and others. My counseling style is relational, supportive, collaborative, and gently challenging.

Somatic Experiencing

A gentle, body-based approach for the treatment of trauma, PTSD, anxiety, and related concerns. It supports the body's innate capacity to heal, and does not require you to re-tell or re-live a traumatic event.

Common questions

How do I get started?

Email me at jamie@jamielcsw.com. I schedule all appointments myself, so I'll reply directly about availability and next steps.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

I am exclusively practicing via telehealth at this time.

Who do you work with?

I work with individuals aged 16 and up on a variety of concerns and goals. You can see my services and areas of specialty for more.

Do you take insurance?

I am an out-of-network provider with all insurance companies. You pay me directly, I provide a "super bill" for services rendered, and you can request reimbursement from your health insurance provider. See Fees & Insurance for questions to ask your insurer.

Do you offer reduced fees?

The accessibility of therapy is important to me. Reduced fee options for psychotherapy services are available on an as-needed basis. Please contact me to discuss fee options and availability.

How long are sessions, and how often would we meet?

Individual sessions are 50 minutes. Most clients meet weekly or every other week in a consistent appointment slot, which is typically the most helpful for therapeutic change.

What is Somatic Experiencing like?

Somatic Experiencing is a gentle, body-based approach that supports the body's innate capacity to heal after overwhelming experiences. It does not require you to re-tell or re-live a traumatic event. Read more about Somatic Experiencing.

What is your cancellation policy?

I ask for 24 hours notice by email or voice mail for cancellations. The full policy is on the Fees & Insurance page.

Ready to get started?

Send me an email with a little about what you're looking for, and I'll get back to you about availability and next steps.
